## Changes made

- All five tracks were transcoded from the album's VBR MP3s (about
  225–294 kbps) to 128 kbps MP3 for web delivery. Cover art and original
  metadata were removed; title, artist, and album tags were added.
- All five play on a continuous loop while their scene is active.
- All five are attenuated at runtime. The boardroom dialogue track is
  additionally ducked beneath character dialogue.
- No melodic, lyrical, or structural changes were made.
