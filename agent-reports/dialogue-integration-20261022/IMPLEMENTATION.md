# Approved dialogue bubble — production implementation

**Date:** 2026-10-22 (afternoon, America/Vancouver)
**Scope:** Nintendo Blue Ocean game (`blue-ocean-game`) only. No deploy, push, paid service, story rewrite, character rename, 3D character/camera change, or runtime dependency was added.
**Authoritative artwork:** `previews/dialogue-bubble-prototype/dialogue-template-clean.png` (3872 × 1552), final approved preview handoff.

## Result

The approved image-backed bubble is now the shared speaking component for every `DialogueLine` phase: opening/narrator, all chapter advisor scenes, consequence/feedback, and boss. It is bottom-anchored in screen space, uses white live name text for every speaker, and has no old nameplate/superellipse/teardrop speech styling left.

Transition and finale are static narrative screens in this game: their story data has no `DialogueLine` and no speaker. They hide the bubble and remain fully clickable. `renderDialogue()` itself is now phase-agnostic, so any future line returned for those phases would use the same component rather than a separate variant.

## Known actual limitations

1. **Transition/finale are not speaker scenes.** They contain authored narrative prose, not `DialogueLine`s, so the bubble is intentionally hidden there. Adding speaker dialogue would require new story content, which was out of scope.
2. **Approved name-fill contrast is below WCAG AA for normal text.** White on the approved blue/green/pink/amber fills measures approximately 2.48:1, 2.80:1, 2.95:1, and 2.94:1; lavender is 3.36:1 and slate 4.68:1. The white-name palette was explicitly approved by the user; the names are large, heavy, shadowed, and inside an ivory tag edge. This is preserved as approved rather than silently recoloured.
3. **Bubble footprint at smaller 16:9 sizes.** Preserving the PNG ratio makes the 751 × 301 px bubble at 1366 and 748 × 300 px at 1024 cover the table and lower character bodies; all speaker faces remain visible. 1920 × 1080 / 1366 × 768 remain the primary projector targets.
4. **Physical projector legibility, GPU frame rate, and 4K remain unmeasured** on this machine. Headless SwiftShader is not representative.
5. No Tripo3D GLB, camera, character-focus, or 3D material behaviour was changed, so their existing validation limits remain as recorded in `BUILD_STATUS.md`.
