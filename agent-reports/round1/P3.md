### Cross-character payoffs

- **Pip → Grumble:** Pip's six-word pitch pays off directly in Grumble's next line: "Pip's pitch is six words and a grin; the invoice has more pages."
- **Bolt → battery/cost:** Bolt's wand, second-screen, dock, and hybrid feedback each raise a physical constraint; Grumble or the coherence line sometimes carries the cost consequence.
- **Mori → nonplayers:** The opening, both 2005 Mori lines, the 2011 mother vignette, and the 2016 travel vignette keep returning to who is absent.
- **Shell → reframe:** Shell's boss questions turn each chosen direction into a value-proposition question instead of a right/wrong judgment.
