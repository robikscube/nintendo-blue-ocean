## Future-GLB sanity note

- All seats and `getSpeakerPosition` derive from the stable root + adapter head anchor, never from mesh-specific dimensions. A future rigged GLB is scaled by the adapter to target height **3.0** with feet at y=0, so the camera contract is unchanged.
- Measured silhouettes are 1.63–2.04 units wide (tail/shell included). Using the projected centre-gap / 1.25 constraint, the current arc accommodates **+22% to +31% wider** silhouettes while still keeping 0.5 body-width gaps (tightest pair: shell–pip, +22%); taller/narrower GLBs are safe. A future asset should be checked against a Box3 width of about **≤2.1 units** at 3.0 height, and re-measured if wider.
- If a future GLB is taller than 3.0, the adapter's `targetHeight / height` scale shrinks it to 3.0; feet are re-centred to y=0, so no floor penetration or camera-clip change. Wider-than-procedural models are the only residual risk.
- No GLBs were added or downloaded; the procedural cast remains the shipped visual.
