## 2. Camera model

### Ensemble wide (verified with P4)
```ts
position [5.0, 6.2, 12.8]
look     [-0.35, 2.4, -1.1]
fov      34.5
```
The constructor now creates the camera with `BOARDROOM_WIDE.fov` (34.5). This fixes P4’s finding that the title/opening path previously stayed at 33 because `focusSpeaker(null)` early-returned.

### Speaker medium shot
Every pose is rebuilt from the live avatar, never hard-coded seats:

- `boardroom.getSpeakerPosition(speaker)` → head/upper-body anchor `(seat.x, 2.46, seat.z)`;
- `avatar.root.getWorldPosition()` + `root.rotation.y` → live seat x/z and facing;
- `avatar.headAnchor` → head height;
- `new THREE.Box3().setFromObject(avatar.root)` → real visual width/height/depth (no synthetic cube);
- a probe `PerspectiveCamera` projects the avatar bounds to screen space and solves camera distance so the **projected silhouette width = 28%** of the frame (target `SPEAKER_SHOT.widthShare = 0.28`, clamp 4.7–10.8);
- camera height = head + 0.55; look target = head − 0.42 (upper chest), so face sits around 43–45% from the top and the table/paws stay visible;
- two 3/4 yaw options either side of facing (18°/29°, centre seat 19°), two/three lens options (32°, 27/28°, 40°), and five small aim offsets (-1.0…+1.0) are scored;
- score penalises: distance deviation from 28%, speaker clipping, any neighbour clipped by >1.2% of frame width, any neighbour whose apparent head size exceeds the speaker (camera-distance ratio > 1.0), and face occlusion;
- the lowest-score candidate wins. The chosen position/look/fov is stored in `speakerFraming` debug info, with candidate details;
- move duration = `clamp(0.6 + travel*0.012, 0.62, 0.78)` s → measured **0.707 s** for the opening Shell move; all camera tweens are killed and rebuilt on every change, so rapid changes cannot stack.

### Wide return and modes
- `focusSpeaker(null)` (narrator, briefing, voting, transition, finale) applies the wide pose over **0.66 s**.
- `showFactory()`/`showBoardroom()` reset the speaker, cue and emphasis; factory presets (`FACTORY_WIDE`/`FACTORY_REVEAL`) and `factoryPose()` are untouched.
- `applyState()` still drives everything; `focusSpeaker`, `applyState`, `showBoardroom`, `showFactory`, `startFactory`, `skipFactory`, `replayFactory`, `getDebugInfo` and `dispose` keep their signatures.

### Speaker cue (scene-director only)
- one shadowless warm `SpotLight` (`0xffe0b5`, intensity 19, angle 0.42, penumbra 0.9) follows the speaker; fades to 0 for wide/factory; set instantly under reduced motion;
- non-speaker avatar materials are multiplied by **0.68** (colour/emissive) and restored for the speaker/wide. Originals are cached per material and restored on dispose. `boardroom.ts` is untouched.
- no postprocessing, screen shake, new assets or extra shadow maps.

### Reduced motion
`applyCameraPose` snaps position/look/fov; the cue light snaps; no camera tweens are created. Verified: camera equals the framed target immediately (`cameraTweens.length === 0`).

## 6. Remaining risks / caveats

1. **Residual neighbour dominance/clipping (the main caveat).** Mori’s best available 3/4 candidate is 1.1% smaller than Grumble’s head; Shell’s is 13% smaller than Pip’s; and Shell/Mori/Pip/Bolt each have one neighbour clipped at a frame edge. The five-seat arc puts a neighbour within roughly ±25–40° at any 28%-width medium shot, so “speaker strictly largest and every neighbour fully inside” is not achievable for all seats without a much tighter shot or removing context. Mitigations: speaker-centred look, 28% width, a warm follow-spot and 0.68 non-speaker dimming. Numbers are reported rather than claiming a clean pass.
2. **Visible body width is at the low end of 20–35%.** Projected silhouette is 28.0%; estimated visible body is ~21–22% (lead’s calibration), and the Mori screenshot reads ~19–20% by eye. If the lead requires a comfortably >20% visible body, the bounded fix is `SPEAKER_SHOT.widthShare 0.28 → 0.30–0.31`; I did not apply it because the file is frozen.
3. **No fresh 1366 screenshots** of the frozen pose; 1366 evidence is metric-only. The 1920 `subset-*` captures were taken immediately before the final material-dimming edit, so they show the frozen geometry but not the dim cue.
4. **GLB avatars:** the solve uses the avatar’s live bounds, head anchor and normalized height 3.0, so a Tripo GLB should frame correctly, but no real GLB was loaded or measured.
5. **4:3 / 1024×768** is not re-authored; the solver keeps the same 28% width at a different aspect by re-solving on resize, but it was not measured this round.
6. **Spotlight/dimming** are subtle scene-layer cues; they were not independently judged on a projector, and material restore-on-dispose is code-reviewed but not visually re-tested.
7. The normal-motion voting-wide check is a headless GSAP artifact; reduced motion proves the exact wide target and the code path is shared.
